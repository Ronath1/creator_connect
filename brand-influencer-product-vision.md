# Product Vision — Brand–Influencer Collaboration Platform

> A fresh-start product brief for use in a new chat. This document describes the product idea, not an existing implementation or an approved technical plan. The final product name has not been chosen.

## What we are building

We want to build a web application that connects brands and product or service owners with social media influencers for promotional collaborations.

Brands can present what they want to promote and how much they are willing to pay. Influencers can discover relevant opportunities, understand the expected work and compensation, communicate with brands, and receive payment through the platform after completing the agreed work under agreed payment conditions.

The central product is a marketplace and workspace for brand–influencer collaborations.

## Vision

Create a shared place where brands can find creators who suit their goals, and creators can find opportunities that suit their content and audiences.

The platform should help both sides build productive relationships, understand their commitments, and see the results of their collaborations.

## Purpose

Make it easier for brands and influencers to discover each other and manage promotional work in one place.

For brands, the purpose is to gain visibility, reach relevant audiences, and understand what their promotional spending achieved.

For influencers, the purpose is to discover paid opportunities, showcase their work, develop brand relationships, and earn money from their creative work and audience.

## Who the application serves

### Brands and product or service owners

Businesses and other entities that want influencers to promote their offerings.

They should be able to:

- Explain their brand and what they want to promote.
- Publish promotional opportunities with clear compensation and expectations.
- Discover and communicate with suitable influencers.
- Manage agreed collaborations and payments.
- Understand the available results from each participating influencer.

### Influencers and content creators

People who create content and operate accounts on social media channels such as Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, Facebook, or X.

They should be able to:

- Present their identity, content, and social media presence.
- Connect social accounts where integrations are supported.
- Browse promotional opportunities and see potential compensation.
- Discuss opportunities and agree on the work with brands.
- Complete promotional work and receive payment through the application.

The social networks are external channels used by creators; they are not separate user roles in the application. Support for particular channels will be decided later.

### Administrators

People responsible for managing the application, supporting users, reviewing problems, and helping keep collaborations trustworthy.

## The core experience

A brand publishes an opportunity → an influencer discovers it → both parties discuss and agree on the work → the influencer completes the promotion → completion and payment are handled under the agreed rules → the brand reviews available results.

The exact application, approval, funding, and payout rules still need to be defined.

## Additional product ideas to carry forward

### Campaign performance and growth

Give brands a section with graphs showing campaign results over time and the reach generated by each influencer.

The aim is to help brands understand individual contributions and assess their promotional spending. The exact measurements depend on the data available from supported platforms. Missing data should be identified clearly, and reach should not be presented as proof of sales.

### Influencer websites

Give influencers an opportunity to create their own public website through the application, where they can present themselves and showcase their work.

Whether this starts as a simple portfolio or becomes a fuller website builder is an open decision.

## What success looks like

- A brand can find an appropriate influencer and arrange a clear promotional collaboration.
- An influencer can find a suitable opportunity, understand the compensation, complete the work, and receive payment.
- Both parties can follow the progress of their collaboration.
- Brands can understand available campaign results without misleading claims.
- Influencers can build a useful public presence and demonstrate their work.

## Fresh-start boundaries

This brief carries forward the product intention only. It does not commit the new project to any previous code, repository, framework, service provider, agent setup, development phase, or deployment arrangement.

Technology, design, first-release scope, payment rules, supported countries, social integrations, and the business model should be decided afresh.

Use this document as the starting context for a new discussion. Additional suggestions should be distinguished from requirements the product owner has explicitly confirmed.
