## Collection APIs do not solve the missing payout contract

PayHere's [Checkout API](https://support.payhere.lk/api-&-mobile-sdk/checkout-api) documents a hosted redirect and server callback checksum; [Refund API](https://support.payhere.lk/api-&-mobile-sdk/refund-api) documents authenticated refund requests. These establish collection/refund tooling, not marketplace approval, beneficiary payments or delayed-release rights. Do not follow documentation examples that send secrets to an online encoder; any later credential handling stays local/server-only.

For any candidate request the exact versioned specification for hosted checkout, signed callbacks, authoritative status retrieval, duplicate handling/idempotency, full/partial refunds, payout initiation/status/returns, disputes and reconciliation exports. Require demonstration of beneficiary eligibility and actual payout failure/retry handling in the appropriate sandbox. A browser success return or merchant bank deposit cannot be recorded as proof that a creator was paid.

## Confirmed direction and remaining implementation dependencies

Owner answers received 17 November:

- CreatorConnect's own business registration is **pending**. No registered platform entity or eligible platform bank arrangement is confirmed.
- There is **no selected provider or merchant account**; the owner intends to create one later. No eligible provider specification or sandbox account/credentials exists yet.
- Brands pay the platform, the platform pays creators and retains a **configurable cut**. The owner wants flexibility based on reasons such as creator type, audience or follower count, and explicitly defers the detailed commission policy. No rate, formula, fee bearer, rounding or effective-date policy is accepted. Existing follower metrics are self-reported; this intent does not authorize automatic fee calculation from unverified counts.

These answers establish direction, not provider approval. Do not request bank numbers, identity documents or secrets in chat/Git.

Confirmed owner clarification, 17 November: intended **brand customers** are mainly Sri Lankan registered businesses, also including individuals and informal/unregistered online, import and distribution businesses. This describes the customer audience, not CreatorConnect's own merchant eligibility. Do not impose a new brand-registration restriction or infer that a provider will onboard the platform from this answer.

Provider confirmation request (draft only, **not sent**): describe CreatorConnect as a Sri Lankan brand–creator services marketplace with business registration pending, intended brand → platform → creator funds flow and an adjustable platform fee whose policy is still deferred. Explain the intended customer mix and creator countries; identify the contracting seller/payee and proposed charge/release events once determined. Ask:

1. Is that precise multi-party model permitted for this entity and account type? Supply written approval, product/contract name and restrictions; a generic merchant-account approval is insufficient.
2. Can creators be individuals as well as registered businesses? Which Sri Lankan banks/currencies/rails are supported, who performs KYC/beneficiary verification, and who may update payout details?
3. Does the provider route funds to beneficiaries, or only settle to our merchant bank? What are the payout API/webhook/status/reference, minimums, fees, limits, return/reversal behavior and reconciliation reports?
4. Can charge/capture and creator transfer be delayed until the agreed work/review event? What authorization expiry, holding limits, reserves and dispute obligations apply? Do not call a delayed payout “escrow” without a verified arrangement.
5. Who funds refunds/chargebacks after merchant settlement or creator payout? Can a transfer be reversed, when is it final, and what negative-balance/reserve responsibilities exist?
6. Supply sandbox access scope and test scenarios without live funds, current rates/fees/FX/taxes as applicable, operational contacts, incident process and exact supported webhook/authentication protocol. No credential is requested until selection/eligibility is resolved.
