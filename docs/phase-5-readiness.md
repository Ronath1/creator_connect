## Host-selection handoff

Compare candidates only when the owner reopens deployment. Record runtime/region, price date/currency/tax/exchange assumptions, quotas and upgrade behavior. Include Next.js Node support (repository requires Node >=22.13), Sharp native decoding, streaming binary 10 MiB uploads plus transport overhead, 120-second request/90-second processing budgets, private TCP access to ClamAV and a supervised cleanup process. The existing `npm start` binds **localhost** for local QA; it is not an internet ingress configuration. Select and verify host-specific binding, proxy/TLS, request/response limits and timeout settings before release. Do not silently reduce bounds or bypass scanning to fit a host.

Record all environment names from [`.env.example`](../.env.example), their owner and rotation procedure, never their values. `NEXT_PUBLIC_*` includes only intended publishable configuration. Clerk secret and attachment worker key stay server-only; the latter is privileged and confined to the narrow attachment worker. No ordinary data request should use it. Configure an exact HTTPS `APP_URL`, Clerk production domain/redirects and the corresponding native Supabase JWT issuer. Fresh signed tokens must prove role and membership behavior on that target; development success is not production trust evidence. Clerk describes separate production instance configuration in its [deployment guide](https://clerk.com/docs/guides/development/deployment/production), checked 16 November.

## Retention and owner decisions

Accepted evidence/audit proposal: retain throughout the pilot and for 90 days after the **owner declares its end**, then review export/deletion or an expressly accepted extension. The pilot end date is unset. Do not create indefinite retention by forgetting that review. No blanket destructive evidence deletion job is provided; deletion/export requests require case review for shared records and an owner-approved process before invitations.

The original “pending uploads cleaned within 24 hours” wording differs from the implemented race-safe behavior: uncertain uploads become eligible **after a 24-hour grace**, then scheduling, exclusive claims and failed-removal retries add delay. Quota remains charged until both objects are confirmed absent. This is not a 24-hour maximum. Owner acceptance of the revised retention wording, user explanation and operational response to prolonged cleanup failure remains required; do not change the accepted scope silently.
